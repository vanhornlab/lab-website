        ### Postdoctoral Researchers
        We encourage inquiries from prospective postdoctoral scholars with experience or strong interest in:
        * **Solution or solid-state NMR spectroscopy** applied to membrane proteins, conformational ensembles, or dynamic regulation
        * **Ion channel biophysics and electrophysiology** (patch clamp, two-electrode voltage clamp, or automated planar patch)
        * **Single-particle cryo-EM** structure determination and conformational landscape analysis
        * **Membrane protein biochemistry**, expression (bacterial, mammalian, or insect cell systems), reconstitution, and functional assays
        * **Computational biophysics and chemoinformatics** (MD simulations, docking, allosteric modeling, and chemical space mapping)

        **To Apply:** Please email **[wade.van.horn@asu.edu](mailto:wade.van.horn@asu.edu)** with:
        1. A cover letter describing your past research accomplishments, future interests, and why you are interested in our lab.
        2. Your curriculum vitae (CV).
        3. Contact information for three professional references.

        ### Graduate Students
        Prospective Ph.D. students should apply through an Arizona State University graduate program:
        * **[School of Molecular Sciences (SMS)](https://sms.asu.edu/graduate-study)** — **[Biochemistry Ph.D.](https://sms.asu.edu/Graduate-Study/Biochemistry-PhD)** or **[Chemistry Ph.D.](https://sms.asu.edu/Graduate-Study/Chemistry-PhD)** programs
        * **[Biological Design Ph.D.](https://degrees.asu.edu/masters-phd/major/ASU00/GCBDSPHD/biological-design-phd;jsessionid=BF40B69211AA53DFBB105EEE4E11F1BF)** program

        **Current ASU Graduate Students:** If you are interested in rotating or joining the lab, please email Wade directly to discuss project availability and schedule a time to meet.

        ---

        ### Undergraduate Researchers
        Undergraduates gain direct, hands-on experience working alongside graduate students and postdocs on protein expression, purification, biophysical characterization, and molecular pharmacology.
        * **Commitment:** We look for students who can commit at least **10–12 hours per week** in the lab for a minimum of **two consecutive semesters** (or summer plus an academic year).
        * **To Apply:** Email **[wade.van.horn@asu.edu](mailto:wade.van.horn@asu.edu)** with:
          * Your resume / CV
          * Unofficial transcript
          * A brief paragraph outlining your scientific interests and future career goals
